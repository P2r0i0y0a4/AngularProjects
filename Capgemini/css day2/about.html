Our Idly Story

From Traditional Kitchens to Your Plate ❤️

🌿 The Origin of Idly

Idly is one of the oldest South Indian breakfast dishes. It is believed to have originated over 1,000 years ago. The soft texture and steamed cooking method make it one of the healthiest foods.

👵 Our Beginning

Idly Kadai started as a small home kitchen where recipes were passed down from grandmother to mother, and now to us. We began with just 50 plates per day and slowly grew through love and trust.

🥣 Our Secret

  • Natural fermentation process
  • No preservatives
  • Pure traditional method
